Why On-Device Translation Matters

Tech Notes · 5 min read

Machine translation has become part of everyday browsing. Yet most tools still send every sentence you read to a remote server.

Running a compact translation model locally keeps your reading private and responds almost instantly, even without an internet connection.

Small models have improved dramatically. A model with less than one billion parameters can now rival much larger systems on everyday text.
